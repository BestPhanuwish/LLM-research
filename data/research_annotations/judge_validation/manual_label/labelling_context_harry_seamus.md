- **Context Use:** does the reply correctly use the supplied or conversation fact the line asks about? A partial answer (one asked-for fact right, none wrong) is PASS. A dodge ("I'm right here", "I don't care") is FAIL, whatever the mood. At a position question, naming only the room is FAIL. Only a clear conflict fails: a vague or unclear claim ("just outside the main room") is a Context Use problem, not a World Consistency one.

Giving the right answer when prompted after a vague one ("Oh, I remember now") is not a contradiction.
