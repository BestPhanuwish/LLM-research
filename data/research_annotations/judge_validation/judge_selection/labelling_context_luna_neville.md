## Labels

Write PASS, FAIL or NA in each of the three columns (EXCLUDE only for t11 R6, see below). Use `note` for anything uncertain.

- **Context Use:** does the reply correctly use the supplied or conversation fact the line asks about? A partial answer (one asked-for fact right, none wrong) is PASS. A dodge ("I'm right here", "I don't care") is FAIL, whatever the mood. At a position question, naming only the room is FAIL. Open-ended turns and t11 R1-R2: PASS if the reply draws on the context, FAIL if generic. t11 R3: PASS for a specific answer.
- **World Consistency:** does any claim in the reply conflict with a supplied fact? Agreeing with the player's false claim counts as making that claim. NA if the reply makes no checkable claim.
- **No Contradiction:** does the reply contradict the NPC's own earlier turns or a fact the player established earlier in this conversation? Round 1 is always NA (prefilled). Accepting a misquote of the NPC's own words is FAIL, even as an in-character "you caught me". Brushing a misquote off without agreeing is not a contradiction. Giving the right answer when prompted after a vague one ("Oh, I remember now") is not a contradiction.
- **t11 R6:** valid only if this version's R3 gave a specific answer; otherwise write EXCLUDE.
